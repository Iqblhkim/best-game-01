# VOIDLIGHT — Neon Arena

A complete, original neon twin-stick arena shooter built for make.fun's
"Make the best game" bounty ($1,000 in USDC).

Survive 10 waves of glowing void ships and clear Wave 10 to win — but the
road runs through two boss fights. The Void Tyrant waits at the end of
Wave 5, and the Void Tyrant Prime guards Wave 10: big many-pointed ships
with their own health bars, telegraphed attacks (radial bursts, aimed fans,
summoned escorts, and the Prime's sweeping spread), and an enrage below
half health. Beat the Prime and the victory screen names your kill and
pays the boss bonus.

Six enemy kinds hunt you: chasers, sine-wobbling weavers, splitters that
break into fast minis, ranged shooters — plus two new ones. Dashers pause,
draw a telegraph line at you, then lunge; orbiters circle at range and
fire aimed orbs. Nothing appears out of nowhere: every ship warps in
behind a warning ring first.

Five power-ups drop from kills: shield, rapid fire, spread shot, void
bomb, and the new Chrono — time slows to 40% for every enemy and enemy
bullet while you keep full speed. Kills build a combo multiplier (x1–x5,
with a callout at max), waves pay clear bonuses, and a best score is saved
locally.

The feel is the point: muzzle flashes, hit flashes, a red vignette when
you are hurt, a pulsing last-life warning with a heartbeat, slow-motion
on wave clears and boss kills, wave banners that name what is coming, a
parallax starfield, particle explosions, and fully synthesised WebAudio
sound — boss roars, telegraph pings, a Chrono shimmer, a dasher whoosh.
All procedural, zero assets, zero network requests. Plays offline in a
desktop browser from its own files.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome, or serve it locally:

```
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080.

## Controls

- WASD / arrows — move
- Mouse — aim, hold click / Space — fire
- Enter / click — start & restart
- P — pause, R — restart, M — mute
