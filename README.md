# VOIDLIGHT — Neon Arena

A complete, original neon twin-stick arena shooter built for make.fun's
"Make the best game" bounty ($1,000 in USDC).

Survive 10 waves of glowing void ships and clear Wave 10 to win — but the
road runs through two boss fights. The Void Tyrant waits at the end of
Wave 5, and the Void Tyrant Prime guards Wave 10: big many-pointed ships
with their own health bars, telegraphed attacks (radial bursts, aimed fans,
summoned escorts, and the Prime's sweeping spread), and an enrage below
half health. Beat the Prime and the victory screen names your kill and
pays the boss bonus.

Seven enemy kinds hunt you: chasers, sine-wobbling weavers, splitters that
break into fast minis, ranged shooters — plus three newer ones. Dashers pause,
draw a telegraph line at you, then lunge; orbiters circle at range and
fire aimed orbs; snipers (from wave 6) hold a long distance, take aim down
a thin telegraph line, then fire one very fast bolt. Nothing appears out of
nowhere: every ship warps in behind a warning ring first.

Six power-ups drop from kills: shield, rapid fire, spread shot, void
bomb, the Chrono — time slows to 40% for every enemy and enemy bullet
while you keep full speed and your own rounds fly 25% faster — and the
new Overdrive: for 8 seconds your rounds pierce through one extra ship
and your trigger runs 40% faster. Kills build a combo multiplier (x1–x5,
with a callout at max), waves pay clear bonuses, and a best score is saved
locally. The end of every run is a report card: accuracy, best combo, run
time, and tyrants destroyed.

The feel is the point: muzzle flashes, hit flashes, a red vignette when
you are hurt, a pulsing last-life warning with a heartbeat, slow-motion
on wave clears and boss kills, wave banners that name what is coming and
count the incoming ships, screen shake on wave clears and boss deaths,
bigger spark bursts when your shots land on a Tyrant, a parallax
starfield, particle explosions, and fully synthesised WebAudio
sound — boss roars, telegraph pings, a Chrono shimmer, a dasher whoosh,
a sniper's rising aim tone and bolt crack, an Overdrive fanfare.
All procedural, zero assets, zero network requests. Plays offline in a
desktop browser from its own files.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome, or serve it locally:

```
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080.

## Controls

- WASD / arrows — move
- Mouse — aim, hold click / Space — fire
- Enter / click — start & restart
- P — pause, R — restart, M — mute
