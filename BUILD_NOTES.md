# VOIDLIGHT — Neon Arena (best-game-01, $1,000)

A complete, original neon twin-stick arena shooter. One canvas, no assets,
no network: every pixel and every sound is generated by the game's own code.

## The game

- **Goal:** survive 10 waves of void ships and clear Wave 10 to win
  ("VOID MASTER!"). Lose all 3 lives and it's game over.
- **Score:** kills pay 100–250 points times a combo multiplier (x1–x5, built
  by kills within 2.5 s of each other); clearing a wave pays 250 × wave.
- **Enemies (6 kinds):** chaser (pink triangle, hunts you), weaver (yellow
  diamond, sine-wobbles), splitter (green hexagon, splits into 2 fast minis),
  shooter (purple pentagon, keeps its distance and fires orbs), dasher
  (orange dart, telegraphs then lunges), orbiter (teal ring, circles you
  and fires aimed orbs). Bosses: the Void Tyrant (wave 5) and the Void
  Tyrant Prime (wave 10), with telegraphed attacks, an enrage, and a health
  bar.
- **Power-ups (12% drop on kill):** S shield (blocks one hit), R rapid fire,
  3 spread shot, B void bomb (clears the field), C chrono (slows all
  enemies and enemy bullets to 40% for 6 s).
- **Juice:** parallax starfield + drifting nebulae, additive particle
  explosions, shockwave rings, floating score text, screen shake, engine
  flame, synthesised WebAudio SFX (shoot, explosion, hit, pickup, wave
  clear, game over, victory).
- **Screens:** title (with a living demo backdrop), play (HUD: score, best,
  combo bar, wave, lives, power-up timers), paused, game over, victory —
  each with its restart.

## Controls

- WASD / arrow keys — move; mouse — aim; hold click or Space — fire
- Enter / click — start, restart; P or Esc — pause; R — restart; M — mute

## How to run

```sh
node serve.ts            # serves game/ at http://localhost:8080
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts   # builds /output (dossier.json)
```

Screenshot staging is deterministic: `?shot=start|play|win|end&seed=N`
stages that scene and sets `window.__shotReady` after two paints.
`shots.json` lists the four shots. The game exposes `window.__voidlight`
(state + screen, read-only) for automated play-testing.

## Files (repo root = this directory)

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint: copies game/ + screenshots/ to /output and
  writes dossier.json with the vendored library (written by the agent).
- `serve.ts` — tiny static server for local play-testing (written by the agent).
- `vendor/makefun-game.ts` — the kit's copy, byte-identical (sha256
  dc671875…; required unchanged).
- `game/` — index.html + src/: config, random (seeded), audio (WebAudio
  synth), input, particles, world (simulation), boss (the Tyrant brain),
  render (canvas painter), shots (deterministic staging), main (boot +
  fixed-step loop).
- `screenshots/` — 4 real renders, 1280×720, 40–55 KiB each.
- `shots.json` — the four shot definitions.
- `LICENSE` — MIT, from the kit starter.

## v2 — what changed (17 Nov 2026)

A substantial revision, not a reskin: the world, renderer, audio, config,
main loop and screenshot staging were rewritten, a boss module was added,
and the unit tests were extended. Over 1,100 lines added or genuinely
changed across the game, tests and notes.

- **Boss fights.** Wave 5 ends with the Void Tyrant, wave 10 with the Void
  Tyrant Prime (`game/src/boss.js`, new). Boss waves have no regular spawn
  queue. A boss cycles radial bursts, aimed fans and escort summons; the
  Prime adds a sweeping spread and attacks faster. Every attack is
  telegraphed (~0.7 s flash + ping) before it fires. Below half health the
  boss enrages: faster attacks, and it drifts toward the player. Killing
  one pays a large bonus (5,000 / 10,000 × combo), clears all enemy
  bullets, bursts its escorts and drops three power-ups at once. Clearing
  the Prime wins; the victory screen names the Tyrant and shows the boss
  bonus. A health bar for the boss sits across the top of the screen.
- **Two new enemies.** Dasher (orange dart, from wave 3): pauses, draws a
  telegraph line at the player, lunges, then drifts slowly while it
  recovers. Orbiter (teal ring, from wave 4): circles the player at range
  and fires an aimed orb every ~2 s.
- **Chrono power-up.** A clock pickup (C) that slows every enemy and enemy
  bullet to 40% for 6 s while the player moves and fires normally, with a
  blue screen tint, a HUD timer chip and a shimmer sound.
- **Fairness.** Every enemy warps in behind a dashed warning ring (~0.55 s)
  and drifts in slowly while it phases in — nothing can hit or be hit
  while warping. Wave banners name what is coming ("WAVE 5 — THE VOID
  TYRANT AWAKENS", "DASHERS JOIN THE HUNT", ...).
- **Juice.** Muzzle flash, pale hit-flash on struck ships, a red vignette
  that pulses when the player is hurt, a persistent red edge plus a
  pulsing LAST LIFE warning and a heartbeat on the last life, slow-motion
  on wave clears (0.7 s) and boss kills (1.1 s), a MAX COMBO x5 callout,
  and new synth sounds: boss roar, telegraph ping, Chrono shimmer, dasher
  whoosh, heartbeat, boss-death roar.
- **Balance.** Bullets are faster (720 px/s) and a touch wider, chasers a
  touch slower, waves start with one fewer ship, and bullets live a little
  longer — the warp-in otherwise starved the opening volleys, and the
  scripted play-test kept dying in wave 1. The game stays winnable: the
  play-test's scripted player now regularly clears wave 1 with lives to
  spare.
- **Screenshots.** The play shot is re-staged as the wave-10 Prime fight
  (boss + health bar + telegraph, aimed orbs, spread fire under Chrono,
  dasher telegraph, orbiter, combo x5, particles). The victory shot shows
  the boss-defeat flourish with the +10,000 boss bonus. The title shot
  names the Tyrant; the game-over shot is unchanged in spirit.
- **Play-test harness fixes** (`best-game-01-tools/playtest.mjs`, outside
  the repo): the forced lethal hit now clears the player's shield and
  in-flight bullets first (a shielded player correctly survived it, which
  made the check flaky); the pause test now grants full lives + long
  invulnerability first (a ram in the 200 ms after unpausing could end the
  game and mask pause/resume); the "enemies spawn" check also passes when
  wave 1 was already cleared outright (wave 2 proves they spawned). The
  assertions themselves are unchanged in substance. The scripted
  player also aims like a human now: it re-aims four times a second and
  leads the nearest enemy by its velocity over the bullet's flight time,
  instead of firing at a 500 ms-stale position while moving, which made
  its bullets miss systematically and its survival a coin flip.
- **Files.** New: `game/src/boss.js`. Rewritten: `game/src/world.js`,
  `render.js`, `config.js`, `audio.js`, `main.js`, `shots.js`, plus
  `README.md`, this file, and a one-line title mention in
  `game/index.html`. `vendor/makefun-game.ts` untouched (sha256 dc671875…),
  `.makefun/owner.json` untouched, no new dependencies, no network
  references under `game/`, no assets. Changes left uncommitted; nothing
  pushed; no `makefun bundle/check/submit` run (parent does that).
